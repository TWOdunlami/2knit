Classic Cowl

• Cast on 100 stitches (Increase based on desired loose fitting.).

• Join your work in the round(be careful to not twist the stitches.). Stitches should all face upward(away from you).

• Row 1-7: * K1, p1; rep from * to end of row. This section is optional you can knit the entire project in stockinette stitch.

• Row 8-60 : work in stockinette stitch until the piece measures approximately 22 cm long.

• Rows 61-67: * K1, p1; rep from * to end of row.

• Bind off loosely.

• Weave in the ends and block.

• Blocking consist of wetting your fabric and letting it dry. It allows the fabric to "relax".

⚘ Tools ⚘

☑ 5mm circular needles
☑ 1 skein of DK worsted yarn
☑ 4 place markers
☑ Two needle toppers
☑ 1 tapestry needle, 1 measuring tape
☑ A good long show/movie/docu-series
☑ Patience and a clear mind.


⚘ Pattern Notes ⚘

• This pattern should be completed using circular needles and worked in the round from the top down
• You can use any Yarn size, but you must adjust your cast on stitches(100 for size-4 dk yarn) to fit you.
• To meet guage use the following equation. Determine how many stitches are needed for 1.5 inches, whch is 9sts.
• Then multiply 9 by the circumference, 16, of your neck & ease(1 inch) and divide 144 by 1.5 it should equal 96, see example below:
9 * 16 / 1.5 = 96


⚘ Terms ⚘

☆ Blocking consist of wetting your fabric and letting it dry. It allows the fabric to "relax".
☆ Gauge Swatch is knitting a 4"x4" square to help with measurements. Generally done when using an alternative yarn.
☆ Stockinette stitch is a uniform clean pattern you commonly see in all knitted sweaters.
☆ Bind off is completing your knitting by casting off your stitches from your needles. This is before weaving in your stitches and wetting your work.
